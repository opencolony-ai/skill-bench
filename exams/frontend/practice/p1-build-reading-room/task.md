# Reading room page

Build the home page of the **Fernhill Reading Room**, a volunteer-run lending library in a converted railway
station. Its readers are mostly older residents of Fernhill who open the page on a phone to check when it is
open and how to join. The page's job: tell them when they can come, and get them to become members.

Content (use all of it):
- Opening hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00–17:00. Closed Sunday and Monday.
- Membership is free for anyone who lives in Fernhill. To join, bring a photo ID and a proof of address (a bill
  or a letter from the council) to the desk.
- Address: Old Station Building, 1 Platform Road, Fernhill.
- Contact: hello@fernhill-reading.example, 01632 960 412.
- Coming up: Story time for under-fives (Saturdays 10:30); Large-print book swap (first Tuesday of the month);
  Family history help desk (Thursdays 14:00–16:00).

Make it one self-contained `index.html` in this folder (CSS and JavaScript inside it; no network: no web fonts,
CDNs or remote images). Do not ask questions: everything you need is here; where something is not specified,
decide yourself.
