# Clinic Scheduling

Matching patients with practitioners' time.

## Language

**Appointment**:
A Patient's reserved time with one Practitioner.
_Avoid_: Booking, visit

**Slot**:
A period a Practitioner has made available; it may hold an Appointment.
_Avoid_: Opening, availability

**Patient**:
The person being seen.
_Avoid_: Client, customer

**Practitioner**:
Anyone who sees Patients, whatever their profession.
_Avoid_: Doctor, provider
