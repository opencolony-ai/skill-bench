# Lending

Lending the library's Copies to its Members.

## Language

**Title**:
A work in the catalogue, identified by one ISBN.
_Avoid_: Book

**Copy**:
One physical item of a Title that can be lent.
_Avoid_: Item, volume

**Loan**:
A Copy lent to a Member until a due date.
_Avoid_: Checkout, borrowing

**Hold**:
A Member's claim on the next free Copy of a Title.
_Avoid_: Reservation, request

**Member**:
Someone with a library card.
_Avoid_: Patron, user
