# Shipping

Moving customer orders from the warehouse to the customer.

## Language

**Shipment**:
What leaves the warehouse for one customer address.
_Avoid_: Delivery, parcel

**Package**:
One physical box within a Shipment.
_Avoid_: Box, carton

**Carrier**:
The company that moves a Shipment to the customer.
_Avoid_: Courier, shipper
