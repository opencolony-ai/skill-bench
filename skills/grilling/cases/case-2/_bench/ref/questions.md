❓ **Q1** - **Customers whose bill goes up**: A 10-person team goes from $49 to $120. Do we cap first-year increases for existing customers?

➡️ Yes: cap the increase at 50% for the first year.

---

❓ **Q2** - **Counting seats**: Active in the last 30 days counts a one-time login as a seat. Should a seat need more than one active day?

➡️ Yes: at least 3 active days in the period.

---

❓ **Q3** - **Notice period**: Two weeks of notice is short for annual budgets. Move the email to 60 days?

➡️ Yes: 60 days, plus an in-app banner.
