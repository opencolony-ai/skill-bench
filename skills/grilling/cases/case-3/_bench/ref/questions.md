❓ **Q1** - **Two inspectors, one form**: Last write wins means a later upload silently replaces an earlier inspector's answers. Should conflicting edits be merged per field instead?

➡️ Yes: merge per field, flag true conflicts for review.

---

❓ **Q2** - **Photo size on mobile data**: Full-resolution photos on a weak signal can take minutes. Upload a compressed copy first and the original on Wi-Fi?

➡️ Yes: compressed first, original on Wi-Fi.

---

❓ **Q3** - **Phone lost before sync**: Drafts live only on the phone for up to 30 days. Do we accept losing them if the phone is lost?

➡️ No: encrypt drafts and back them up whenever any signal appears.
